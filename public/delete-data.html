Delete your data (keep your account)

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This page explains how to delete specific data in the Ragly app without deleting your entire account.

To delete your whole account and all associated data, see Delete your Ragly account.

How to delete data in the Ragly app

Open Ragly and sign in, then use the steps below.

Delete a reminder

  1. Open the reminder you want to remove.
  2. Tap Delete reminder.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Delete a vaccine record

  1. Open the vaccine you want to remove.
  2. Tap Delete vaccine.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Delete a topic / health record or note

  1. Open the topic (health record) or note you want to remove.
  2. Tap Delete (topic or note).
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Delete a pet profile

  1. Open the pet profile → Edit.
  2. Tap Delete / remove pet.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Deleting a pet permanently removes that pet's vaccines, reminders, and topic records. You must keep at least one pet if your account still has pets.

Request help by email

  1. Email support@ragly.cloud from your Ragly account email.
  2. Subject: Ragly data deletion request.
  3. Describe which data you want deleted (for example specific pets, reminders, notes, or photos) while keeping your account.
  4. We will respond and complete verified requests within 30 days.

What data can be deleted this way

  • Reminders
  • Vaccination records and related photos
  • Topics / health records and notes (and related photos)
  • Pet profiles and pet photos

What is kept if you only delete some data

  • Your Ragly account and email sign-in
  • Any data you choose not to delete
  • Subscription/transaction records held by Google Play or Apple, if you have a subscription (managed in the store)

In-app deletions are applied promptly. Email requests are completed within 30 days after we verify the request. Limited legal or security records may be retained where required by law.
