Ragly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Ragly ("we", "our", or "us") is a pet-care organization app operated by the Ragly team based in Israel. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use the Ragly mobile application and related services.

Contact for privacy requests: support@ragly.cloud

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Ragly, we may collect:

  • Account information — email address and authentication data (for example one-time codes and sign-in tokens).
  • Pet and care records you choose to enter — pet name, breed, age, weight, sex, vaccinations, health notes, reminders, and related details. These are personal notes you enter; they are not clinic medical records and are not used to diagnose or treat animals.
  • Photos and files you upload — for example pet photos or vaccination proof images.
  • Device and app information — push notification tokens, timezone, notification preferences, basic device/app diagnostics, and crash-related data needed to keep the Service reliable.
  • Analytics data — limited app usage events via Firebase Analytics (for example feature use and stability) to improve the product.
  • Purchase information — if you subscribe, purchase status and entitlement data via our payment partners. We do not store your full payment card number.

2. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account
  • Provide app features (profiles, records, reminders, notifications)
  • Send verification emails and support replies
  • Deliver push notifications for reminders you set (when enabled)
  • Process subscriptions and restore purchases
  • Improve reliability, security, and customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Service providers / third parties

We use trusted providers to operate Ragly. They process data only to provide their services to us:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, analytics/diagnostics, and file storage
  • MongoDB Atlas — application database
  • Resend — transactional email (for example OTP codes)
  • Expo — app delivery and push notification routing
  • RevenueCat / Google Play / Apple — subscription management (where applicable)
  • Cloud hosting providers — running our API (for example Google Cloud)

4. Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device push token and your timezone so we can send reminder alerts you schedule. You can turn notifications off in Ragly Settings and in your device system settings at any time.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. If you delete your account (in Settings or by emailing support@ragly.cloud), we delete or anonymize associated personal data within 30 days, unless we must keep limited records for legal, security, or accounting reasons (for example subscription receipts handled by Apple or Google).

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, update, or delete your personal data. You can:

  • Access or update information in the app where available
  • Control notification preferences in Settings
  • Request account or data deletion via support@ragly.cloud or in-app account deletion where available

We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable time.

7. Children's privacy

Ragly is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided us personal data, contact support@ragly.cloud and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data (including encrypted transport and access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. International processing

Your information may be processed in countries where we or our providers operate, including outside Israel. We take steps designed to protect personal data in line with this policy.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Ragly after an update means you accept the revised policy.
