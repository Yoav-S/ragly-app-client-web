Delete your Ragly account

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Ragly account and associated data. Ragly is a pet-care mobile application.

How to request account deletion

Option A — In the Ragly app (recommended)

  1. Open the Ragly app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Option B — By email

  1. Send an email to support@ragly.cloud from the email address linked to your Ragly account.
  2. Use the subject line: Ragly account deletion request.
  3. Include the account email address and a clear request to delete your Ragly account and associated data.
  4. We will confirm the request and complete deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your Ragly account is deleted, we delete or remove access to:

  • Your account and authentication information (including email)
  • Pet profiles and related details you added
  • Vaccination records, health notes, and reminders
  • Photos and files you uploaded
  • Notification preferences and push tokens
  • App account data stored in our systems for your user

What may be retained

  • Limited records required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes (for example subscription/transaction records kept by Google Play or Apple under their policies)
  • Aggregated or anonymized information that no longer identifies you

Unless a longer retention is required by law, we complete account and personal data deletion within 30 days of a verified request. Backup systems may take a short additional period to fully purge residual copies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Ragly account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play or App Store subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in the store account used for the purchase.
